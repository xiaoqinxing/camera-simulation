## Sources and body coverage

These are real photogrammetry scans captured with a 112-camera rig, according to the [author's release article](https://huggingface.co/blog/IvoDRL/hsrd-100). Every selected model includes the head, torso, arms, legs and footwear. Original posed bodies and photographed surface color are retained. The supplied LOD1 mesh is approximately 100,000 faces with an 8192×8192 diffuse texture; there is no generated body or face replacement.

## Adaptations

Conversion uses `scripts/build-hsrd-scans.py`:

1. Import the author's original LOD1 OBJ and bind the supplied photographed diffuse texture. Preserve the source UVs, complete posed body, expression and clothing.
2. Orient the model for the Three.js/glTF Y-up scene, facing generally toward +Z. Apply a uniform scale to a 1.75 m scene height, with the bottom at floor Y=0.
3. Keep all source LOD1 triangles. No additional mesh decimation, body cropping, face replacement, body synthesis, retouching, skin recoloring or texture upscaling is applied.
4. Retain the supplied 8192×8192 texture dimensions, encode it as JPEG quality 92, and embed it in the self-contained GLB. Neutral roughness supports real-time studio lighting.
5. Render the adapted GLB for previews and verify full body coverage and texture alignment. Exact output statistics and hashes are recorded by the converter.

The distributed GLBs retain their 8K textures. For browser memory limits, Studio resizes decoded scan images to a maximum edge of 4096 pixels, or 2048 pixels with software rendering, when the browser supports bitmap resizing. These in-memory copies preserve aspect ratio and photographed color; they do not modify the files. Browser previews and photo exports therefore do not promise 8K texture sampling.

Original scan textures contain captured lighting; they are useful photographic subjects, not calibrated reflectance measurements. Preserve this attribution, license, source links and modification record when redistributing. Further adaptations must also indicate their modifications.
